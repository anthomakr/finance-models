# Finance Models

> Forkable financial models you can actually keep. Open, readable, one click into [Layerz](https://layerz.cc).

Most financial models are disposable: every deal, every budget starts from a blank sheet, and the logic is tangled up with the data so you can't audit, version or reuse it. The models here are the opposite. Each one is **structured, balanced, and reproducible** (the balance sheet ties out every year), and each opens straight into Layerz where you can fork it for free and make it your own.

**We share the recipe, not the black box.** Every model page shows its structure, its conventions and its real numbers.

## How forking works

1. **Open a model in Layerz** (free account).
2. **Fork it**: you get a model you own, fully editable.
3. **Edit the drivers** and watch every statement recompute. The balance check stays at 0.

No black box, no lock-in. The export to Excel is always clean.

## Start your own library

This repo is a **GitHub template**. If you teach or create around AI and finance, you can have a library exactly like this one, **under your own name**: your models, on your GitHub, opening into Layerz.

[![Use this template](https://img.shields.io/badge/Use_this_template-Start_your_library-2D6BFF?logo=github)](https://github.com/anthomakr/finance-models/generate)

That's the whole point: your credit, your audience, real artifacts your people can fork and keep instead of prompts they'll lose. And you don't start from an empty folder, you inherit the engine: the [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) in this repo tells any capable agent how to render a model page straight from your live Layerz model over MCP, and refresh it in one command. Point your agent at it, add your models, done.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the two ways in: get a model featured here, or spin up your own library.
