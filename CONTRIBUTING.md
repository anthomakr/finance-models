# Add a model

This repo is a small, curated library of forkable Layerz models. There are two ways to be part of it.

## Get your model featured here

You built something clean in Layerz and want it in this library:

1. Make sure the model **balances** (balance check = 0) and its FINANCE.md documents the purpose, structure and conventions.
2. Get its **public share link** from Layerz.
3. Open an issue with the link and a one-line description, or reach out directly.

Each model gets its own page: a readable recipe, the structure as a diagram, the real numbers, and an **Open in Layerz** button.

## Want your own library like this?

If you teach or create content around AI and finance, you can have a library like this **under your own name** — your models, on your GitHub, opening into Layerz, powered by the same structure.

That's the whole point: your credit, your audience, real artifacts your people can fork and keep instead of prompts they'll lose. Reach out and we'll build the first model with you.
